                # Description
                This probably falls under your words, " input on other aspects of the Personal Space Weather Station is also welcome". This feature is what I am currently working on in prep for AGU MacGyver. It is to use automated ray tracing (PyLap at an elevation increment of 0.005?) with a small suite of python scripts I've written / am writing to generate synthetic spectrograms. These spectrograms have the climatology of what rays can propagate from WWV to station X on frequency Y and what Doppler they have. I then know exactly what mode results in what Doppler. My concept is that the synthetic spectrogram would be overlaid (or underlaid) with the measured spectrogram to help elucidate the modes present, find missing modes, show weather vs climate etc etc.
