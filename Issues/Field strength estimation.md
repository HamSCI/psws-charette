
# Field strength estimation
## Description
RX888 Mk 2 receivers with WsprDaemon and ka6q-radio software are able to make measurements of the signal levels and, with new code, noise levels at the SMA input to the receiver. But between that SMA connector and the incoming signals there will be site-specific antennas, filters, preamps, splitters etc making the task of estimating the field strength of the incoming signals difficult.
Might there be a few reference sites where the equipment between the RX888 SMA and free space is a) as simple as possible and b) as well calibrated as possible such that these sites could provide field strength estimates? For example a few sites might be equipped with a Rohde and Schwarz HE010 antenna with its well-known calibration. At those sites such an installation could become a 'transfer standard' to calibrate co-located systems e.g. the DX Engineering 'monopole' active antenna.
